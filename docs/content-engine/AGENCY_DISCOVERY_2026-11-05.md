## 1. Strategic positioning (confirmed)
- PoStory is a **done-for-you managed content/marketing delivery service**, **not SaaS** sold to the agency or end customer.
- The agency owns the client relationship, manages client communication, conducts original on-site photography/video, obtains approvals and coordinates account authorization.
- PoStory organizes the materials into content strategy, copywriting, marketing imagery, publishing schedule, approvals, publication status and monthly report.
- **Do not make the client-facing value proposition about AI** (e.g., “AI automatically writes copy”). Say “文案撰写 / copywriting”, “内容策划 / content planning”, “营销图片设计 / creative design”. Internally, AI-assisted tools may be used; never misrepresent genuinely generated visuals as client photos.
- Do not criticize an agency's existing filming or claim PoStory knows their customer better. Show usable alternative deliverables, give the agency choice, and keep subjective diagnostic scores internal.
- Do not contact or solicit an agency's end customers. Coordinate **only with the agency**. Preserve approvals and the existing agreement on content and account ownership.

## 2. Custom proposal and scope (confirmed discussion, not an executed contract)
The bespoke agency proposal is **C$599/month/store**:
- **20 distinct content units/updates per month**, scheduled across a **30-day operating calendar**.
- Agency supplies the real photo/video material. PoStory performs copywriting, visual design/format adaptation, content package preparation, scheduling, publishing (subject to platform permission), and monthly reporting.
- **Two consolidated revision rounds per month**, collected and sent by the agency. Additional rounds, new work, complete concept resets and special video work are priced separately.
- Do **not** promise “16 images + 4 edited videos”, “four videos per month”, “weekly video”, or a fixed video length. The agency already has a filming service. Existing video assets may be scheduled or repackaged only within a mutually accepted scope.
- An item posted on multiple supported platforms is one **content unit** for production-count purposes; clarify platform-specific posts/placement counting in the service agreement.
- Independent filming, extensive video editing, ongoing end-customer correspondence, inbox/comment moderation, paid-ad management and ad spend are **not bundled**.
- Paid Meta, TikTok and Google advertising may be a separately priced optional offering.
- Keep this bespoke C$599 proposal separate from the standard agency and owner-facing offers: no accidental cross-pricing or cross-copy. Do **not** propagate a marketing price into billing code without verifying the live contract and product rules.
- Client-facing material should call it simply **“月底报告 / monthly report”**, not “white-label report” or “白牌”.
- Selected agency case industries: **windows and doors, discount appliances, furniture retail**. Do not default to nail salons or restaurants in this bespoke material.

## 4. Four concrete production capabilities
1. **Copywriting:** product/service descriptions, opening hooks, headlines, captions, local-language adaptation, hashtags where relevant, and clear inquiry/booking CTAs. Facts must be approved.
2. **Design with original photography:** crop/reframe, clean up, typographic composition, branding, promotional layouts; **client-shot imagery remains the primary source**.
3. **Creative supporting assets:** approved graphic accents, backgrounds, holiday motifs, offer posters and reusable templates; do not fabricate product inventory, real-world before/after photos, reviews, prices, warranties or installation outcomes.
4. **Platform adaptation:** Facebook, Instagram and TikTok format, title, ratio and scheduling differences, within supported account permissions.

## 5. Four agency objections heard on November 5
### A. “Can existing shoots really extend into 20 posts?”
Create a shot inventory, extract evidence-backed product/use-case facts, and build distinct content pillars (project or product evidence; education; feature/detail; trust; local offer/inquiry). **Twenty is a content-delivery target, not evidence that every shoot contains twenty independent stories.** Flag gaps and request one specific missing asset or fact, without telling the agency their filming is bad.

### B. “Will the results look AI-generated?”
Use real images and verified products, avoid repetitive generic copy, distorted products, exaggerated visual effects, invented testimonials/prices and near-duplicate post layouts. Human review remains mandatory before external use. Keep quality feedback constructive and nonconfrontational.

### C. “Will derived content get reach, leads or better paid-ad results?”
Optimize separate outcomes: worth watching, worth sharing/saving, worth taking action on (especially **qualified local** action). Create several meaningfully different creative **hypotheses** rather than multiple cosmetic copies. Measure actual results after publication; organic reach is not ad results, and neither is a guarantee of leads or sales. Offer a few approved creative candidates for agency-run paid testing, not claims of algorithmic certainty.

### D. “Can we one-click edit the original video?”
Agency wants removal of filler words / retakes, pauses, sensible transitions, framing, subtitles, story selection and review. Distinguish (a) speaking/interview footage from (b) visual-first product/installation/showroom footage. A video with no hook/theme is **not fixed** by auto-captions or a transition. Analyze, propose a story, produce an editable preview, permit timecoded revisions, then export after approval. The filmed evidence must support the story.

## 6. Crucial trust and relationship constraint (confirmed)
The agency and even the end customer might already regard existing footage as good. PoStory must not require the agency to agree the original is poor, accept a diagnostic score, retrain their filmmakers, or defend PoStory as a “marketing expert.” Present:
- **Original** (unaltered)
- **Optional project/showcase alternative**
- **Optional inquiry/paid-ad creative alternative**
Then let the agency select; none is described as inherently better until results support it. Diagnostic grades and shot scores are **internal routing aids**, not customer-facing verdicts. Communicate missing material neutrally (e.g. “to make this product post we need a clear model/price photo”), not “your filming is bad.”

Initially prove **usefulness and reduced effort**, then **reliable delivery**, and only later **marketing lift** with comparable results. Do not claim causation or superiority based on a single post.

## 7. Creative learning hypotheses (not confirmed performance facts)
- Assess content for **watch value**, **save/share value**, **action value**; do not insist all posts maximize all dimensions.
- Internally proposed rubric: opening/hook 25%, useful value 20%, save/share motive 20%, visual pacing 15%, local audience fit 10%, CTA 10%. **Uncalibrated heuristic**, never a viral-likelihood probability or official platform score.
- Draft monthly learning split: **5 / 10 / 5** posts across early test, iteration, and late-cycle learning, contingent on approvals and platform data. It is not a mandatory delivery cadence or guarantee.
- For paid ads, measure impressions, CTR, CPC, CPL, qualified leads, CAC and meaningful sales when attributable and available; report budget, platform, placement, dates and attribution assumptions. Keep paid optimization and ad budget **out of** the base content package.
- A first evidence-building experiment could compare one original agency edit with one project showcase and one inquiry-led alternative, after agency approval, under as comparable conditions as possible. Compare **review effort and acceptance** before asserting reach improvements.

## 8. Editing triage from unstructured real-world footage
Classify each asset set as:
- **A — usable for direct restructure:** enough evidence to create one or more coherent stories.
- **B — usable with missing facts/shot:** produce supported content now and request a precise factual correction or pickup.
- **C — unsuitable for the intended claim/format:** do not invent evidence; offer a text/photo route or request new footage.

A “hook” must be extracted or reconstructed from **actual footage/verified facts**. Before-after must use real matched scenes, technical/product claims require verification, and unsupported inventory/price statements are prohibited.

Shot Planner **before shooting** is an optional future aid, not a prerequisite to working with existing agency footage. The initial offering must not force the agency to change its current workflow.

## 9. Delivery economics and boundaries
The initial C$599 offer should be validated against **actual operator hours**: client onboarding, asset sorting, copy/design production, revisions, video handling, scheduling, authorization failures and reporting. No commitment to full managed creative direction or complex video post-production at the base price. Keep time per unit, number of revision notes, acceptance rate, render cost, retries and margins; decide later whether advanced editing/creative testing requires a separate tier.
