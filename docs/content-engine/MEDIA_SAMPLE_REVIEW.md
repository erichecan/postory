This sounds like on-set riding instruction, not a finished sales voiceover. Mute or replace it for a polished promotional cut unless the client deliberately wants a candid behind-the-scenes feel.

The other short clips produced only isolated words such as “好” in ASR. Treat those as unverified ambient speech or recognition noise, not subtitle copy. Listen to the original sound before deciding whether to retain any ambience.

## Recommended first edit

Make a 12–16 second vertical product showcase:

1. Open with an illuminated riding moment from asset 07 or a strong moment in asset 06.
2. Show four or five fast product-detail shots from assets 01–05; choose distinct colors and avoid giving every near-identical shot equal time.
3. Return to the riding footage from asset 06, then use assets 07 and 08 only if they add a genuinely different angle or light color.
4. Close on a clear product frame with a client-approved store/location/inquiry CTA.

Possible hook to validate: “会发光的平衡车，实际骑起来是什么样？” Only use an on-screen “到店试骑” CTA if the shop confirms that customers can actually test ride.

Use light music or cleaned ambience. Keep captions concise. Use only claims visible in the footage or separately approved by the client. Do not invent speed, range, safety, age suitability, warranty, price, or availability.

## Why not stitch all clips in their supplied order?

Five clips are close-ups and three show similar riding moments. A straight join would repeat the same visual pattern and leave the on-set instruction in the audio. The edit needs a simple arc: **motion hook → product variety → real-use demonstration → local next step**.

One source bundle can support two distinct variants:
- Product/color showcase.
- Riding experience/demo.

It does not support 20 materially different video concepts by itself. For the monthly 20-post package, reuse facts and shots carefully across a mix of photo/carousel, educational copy, product detail, and a small number of video posts. Avoid producing near-duplicates just to hit a count.
